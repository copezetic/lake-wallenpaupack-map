Events around Lake Wallenpaupack

Upcoming events in Hawley, Honesdale and around the lake, each linked to its organiser.

  • Hawley Farmers Market (indoors)Friday, November 27, 2026 · The Hawley Hub, 318 Main Ave, Hawley. Weekly Friday market, indoors from November through April.

Holiday parade down Main Street to Central Park, where the tree and the Irving Cliff star are lit. Rain date November 28; the borough's own calendar lists the 28th, so check before you go.
  • Small Business Saturday and Tree LightingSaturday, November 28, 2026 · Downtown Hawley. Shopping day downtown, ending with the borough's tree lighting.

  • Visit with SantaFriday, December 11, 2026 · Lake Wally Cafe, 401 Main Ave, Hawley. Santa meets children at the cafe across the Winterfest weekend.

  • Winterfest Artisan FairSaturday, December 12, 2026 · Hawley Silk Mill, 8 Silk Mill Dr. Two-day indoor fair of local makers.
  • Christkindl MarketSaturday, December 12, 2026 · Wallenpaupack Brewing Co., 73 Welwood Ave, Hawley. Holiday vendor market at the brewery.
  • Road to Bethlehem live nativitySaturday, December 12, 2026 · Hawley United Methodist Church, 315 Church St. Free guided walk through seven live nativity scenes.
