Groceries, beer and liquor around Lake Wallenpaupack

Where to stock up around Lake Wallenpaupack: grocery stores, beer distributors, and the state wine and spirits stores. In Pennsylvania, liquor is sold at state Fine Wine & Good Spirits stores and cases of beer at beer distributors.
