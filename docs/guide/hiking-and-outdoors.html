Hiking and the outdoors at Lake Wallenpaupack

Where to hike and get outside around Lake Wallenpaupack, from the organisations that look after each place.

Open the 3D map

Places to hike

  • Shuman Point Natural AreaA loop of about three miles through woods along the shore, off Route 590.
  • Ledgedale Natural AreaEighty acres with four marked trails, off Kuhn Hill Road.
  • Lacawac SanctuaryMore than 12 miles of trails, open sunrise to sunset.
  • Bruce Lake Natural AreaState forest land beside Promised Land State Park.
  • Promised Land State ParkState park at the south edge of the map.
  • Dorflinger-Suydam Wildlife SanctuaryTrails at White Mills. No pets.
  • Prompton State ParkTwenty-six miles of trails around Prompton Lake, west of Honesdale.
  • Bingham Park and the river walkHawley's borough park, on the Lackawaxen.

Winter

  • Snowmobiling at Promised LandMore than 23 miles of designated trails on park and state forest land, open from the day after the last deer season in December to April 1 when there is snow.
  • Skiing and snowshoeing at Promised LandAll trails are open to cross-country skis and snowshoes; the park office lends snowshoes when there are at least six inches of snow.
  • Skiing and snowshoeing at PromptonTrails are open to cross-country skis and snowshoes, with sledding and ice fishing too.
  • Downhill: Ski Big Bear at MasthopeThe nearest ski hill, at Lackawaxen, east of Hawley and off this map. Skiing and snow tubing.

On the ice

  • Nobody checks the lake's iceThe Fish and Boat Commission has said the ice on Lake Wallenpaupack is not monitored by any authority, and that there is no such thing as safe ice.
  • State parks' rule of thumbAt least four inches of solid ice for one person and seven for a small group, and the ice near shore is weaker. This is general guidance, not a reading for this lake.
