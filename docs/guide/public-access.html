Public beaches and boat launches on Lake Wallenpaupack

Most of the shoreline belongs to private communities and homes. These are the places open to anyone.

Open the 3D map

Swim

  • Palmyra Township Public BeachThe public swimming beach, on Route 6 near the dam, with picnic space and parking. Listed as open daily 11 am to 5 pm, weather permitting, from Memorial Day weekend to Labor Day weekend. Sources disagree on admission, so check before you go.

Launch a boat, kayak or canoe

  • Mangan Cove Access AreaPennsylvania Fish and Boat Commission launch off Route 590, close to Route 6. Free to launch, with trailer parking and a handicap-accessible fishing pier. Position on this map is approximate.
  • Wilsonville Recreation Area113 Ammon Drive, Hawley. Launch and campground run by the dam's owner; there is a launch fee, waived for campers. Pump-out for a fee.
  • Caffrey Recreation Area431 Lakeshore Drive, Lakeville. Launch and campground; launch fee, waived for campers. Pump-out for a fee.
  • Ironwood Point Recreation Area155 Burns Hill Road, Greentown. Launch and campground; launch fee, waived for campers. Handicap-accessible fishing pier.
  • Ledgedale Recreation Area263 Ledgedale Road, Greentown. Launch and campground at the quiet upper end of the lake; launch fee, waived for campers. Kayak rentals.

Fish from shore

The two handicap-accessible fishing piers, at Mangan Cove and Ironwood Point, are the shore spots a current source confirms.

    Walk by the water

    • Wallenpaupack Lake TrailA packed-gravel path along the dike and shore near the dam, passing the public beach. The township gives about 1.8 miles from the dike to the Environmental Center; the visitors bureau describes about three miles in all.
    • Wallenpaupack Creek TrailA 1.2-mile out-and-back below the dam, starting from the Environmental Learning Center, with parking there. Open seasonally and closed in winter; no pets or bicycles.
    • Shuman Point Natural AreaMore than 300 acres of undeveloped shoreline with a blue-blazed loop of 3 to 3.5 miles. Free, leashed dogs welcome, no facilities. Parking is on Route 590 in Lakeville.
    • Ledgedale Natural Area80 acres with four marked trails and parking off Kuhn Hill Road. Dawn to dusk, leashed dogs. Hiking only: fishing and picnicking are not allowed.

    Start here

    • Lake Wallenpaupack Visitors Center2512 Route 6. Listed as open daily 9 am to 5 pm, with a free spotting scope and fishing tackle to borrow.

    Gathered from the sources named; nobody has visited each place for this map. Fees, hours and rules change, and launch fee amounts were not published where we looked. The recreation areas belong to the dam's owner, whose own site has the current terms.
